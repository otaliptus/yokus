# Yokuş

A short, wordless, cozy 3D pixel-art walk up a Central Anatolian village road.

**Play live:** https://yokus.otaliptus.com

## About

You step off the village minibus at the foot of the hill and walk slowly uphill through seven memories of a summer afternoon in Central Anatolia:

1. **Arrival** — The dolmuş drops you off at the edge of the village road.
2. **Tea House** — Sit with the village uncles over tavla, drop sugar cubes, stir with a spoon, and sip Turkish tea while the çaycı keeps watch.
3. **Football Pitch** — Curl drag-from-ball free kicks past the jumping wall and diving keeper.
4. **The River** — Borrow the spare bamboo rod by the stone bridge, cast into the stream, and drop fresh river trout into the bucket.
5. **Tandır Courtyard** — Eat warm blistered flatbreads straight from the clay tandır oven with your aunt.
6. **Great-Grandfather's House** — Meet the three courtyard cats (a grumpy tail-lashing black cat, an orange biscuit-maker, and an aloof white cat) and step through the blue door.
7. **The Reading Room & Dusk** — Pick a book, sit by the window as evening falls over the village, and stay for unlimited free kicks, tea, or bread.

## Controls

- **Click / Tap** glowing items, NPCs, or entrance/exit arrows to interact.
- **Drag** during walks or roadside pauses to look around in 360°.
- **Scroll / Pinch / `+` `-` `0`** to zoom in and out.
- **Free Kicks:** Pull back from the ball like a slingshot (slide back to cancel), or drag directly toward the goal.
- **`M`** to toggle mute.

## Run Locally

The game is pure static HTML/CSS/ES modules with Three.js vendored under `vendor/three/`:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
# then open http://localhost:8000
```
